How to Check JAMB Result Online in 2025 (Simple Guide for All Candidates)

By TrendNest, Contributor.

Published August 08, 2025, 8:45 PM

If you wrote the JAMB exam in 2025, the first thing on your mind is how to check your result. Luckily, it’s now very easy to check online using your registration number or phone number. Here's a simple guide you can follow.

No scratch card needed. Just your JAMB Reg Number or the phone number you used to register.

1. Go to the Official JAMB Result Portal

Visit the official JAMB result portal for 2025. This is the safest and fastest way to check your score.

2. Enter Your JAMB Registration Number

Type in your 10-digit registration number or the phone number linked to your JAMB profile.

3. Click on “Check My Result”

Once you click it, your result will display on the screen. Make sure your internet connection is stable so it loads correctly.

4. Print or Screenshot Your Result

After checking, you can print it out or screenshot it for your records. You’ll need it for admission or screening.

5. If You See “No Result Yet”

This may happen if your result hasn't been uploaded yet or if your exam was under investigation. Don’t panic — just check again later.

6. What If You Forgot Your Reg Number?

If you don’t remember your registration number, use the phone number you used during registration. Or go back to your email or printout to retrieve it.

Final Words

Checking your JAMB result online is now very simple. No stress, no scratch cards, no confusion. Just follow the steps above and you’re good to go.

Whether you passed well or not, remember — your result is just one part of your story. Keep moving forward.
