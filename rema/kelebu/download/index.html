Rema – Kelebu

By TrendNest, Contributor.

Published September 11, 2025, 08:19pm

Artist Name: Rema

Track Title: Kelebu

Recorded: 2025 Music

Country: Naija Music

Category: Latest Music

Nigerian superstar Rema drops another hot jam titled “Kelebu”, a track that blends Afrobeat vibes with fresh energy. This 2025 release is set to take over playlists and clubs across Africa and beyond.

Rema, one of Nigeria’s most celebrated Afrobeat stars, has once again delivered a track that will set the airwaves on fire. His new release “Kelebu” combines smooth melodies with vibrant Afrobeat rhythms, giving fans a taste of his unique sound and creative energy.

The single reflects Rema’s ability to stay ahead in the music scene, blending local vibes with international appeal. Known for hit songs like “Calm Down” and “Soundgasm”, Rema continues to prove why he remains one of the most consistent young stars in African music.

When Rema released his latest track “Kelebu,” he gave fans more than just a new sound to vibe to. The superstar announced the Kelebu Dance Challenge, promising to reward the winners with a grand prize of $10,000. Thousands of fans participated, but it was the energetic Pop Boys crew who impressed the most and eventually took home the prize.
To make it even more exciting, Rema didn’t just reward them — he also appeared alongside the Pop Boys in the official Kelebu music video, showing off his own dance moves. This moment made the release of “Kelebu” not just about music, but about community, fun, and celebrating talent.
