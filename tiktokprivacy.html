Depending on the features you use, we may process the following categories of information:

We use information only as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain requested features, including connecting accounts, identifying the connected profile, preparing or submitting video uploads, showing publishing status, troubleshooting errors, protecting the Service, and responding to support requests.
